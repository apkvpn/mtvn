## Information processed

Manoto VPN does not require an account. The app stores imported VPN profiles and local preferences on the device. The app requests VPN and network permissions to create the encrypted tunnel and to test server latency.

When connected, network traffic is routed through the VPN endpoint selected by the user. That endpoint may process traffic and connection metadata under its own policies. Manoto VPN does not sell, inject advertisements into, or intentionally inspect the contents of user traffic.

## QR codes, clipboard, and imported links

QR codes, clipboard text, and configuration links are used to create a local VPN profile when the user chooses an import action. They are not uploaded by the app unless the user explicitly uses a third-party service.

## Sharing and retention

Manoto VPN does not sell personal information. Local profiles remain on the device until deleted. The operator of a selected VPN endpoint may retain connection or traffic data; users should review that operator's policy before connecting.
